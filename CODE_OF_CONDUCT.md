# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

AISciHub OSS is a free, youth-led, cross-school community where developers can
build software, learn from working together, and contribute at their own pace.
Treat other participants with respect regardless of experience, background,
identity, school affiliation, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific in discussions and code reviews. Critique
  ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience. Respect project
  maintainers' decisions, contributor boundaries, and time.
- Give appropriate credit to public projects and their contributors.
  Respect licenses and do not misrepresent someone else's work.
- Use normal GitHub collaboration channels and keep conversations focused
  on software. Share only information needed for the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, bullying, discrimination, sexual comments, threats,
  intimidation, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Asking young participants for personal details, private contact
  information, school details, photos, schedules, or off-platform contact.
- Posting another person's private information, secrets, private files,
  or communications without permission.
- Spam, targeted disruption, impersonation, plagiarism, or pressure to join
  a school, program, commercial service, or activity.

## Scope and reporting

These standards apply to the community repository and interactions in the
community's GitHub spaces. Listed projects remain independently governed.

If a concern can be described without sensitive or identifying details, use
the community feedback form when available or draw a maintainer's attention
to the existing public GitHub interaction. For harassment, threats, private data, or other
sensitive incidents, use
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting options](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not post private evidence in a public issue. If a concern involves a
maintainer, bring it to another maintainer or to GitHub instead.

## Enforcement

Community maintainers will handle concerns fairly and without collecting
personal dossiers. Depending on the situation, they may request a correction,
hide or remove content using GitHub's moderation tools, lock discussions,
restrict participation in community spaces, or remove a catalog listing.
They may also report conduct through GitHub's platform reporting process.
Avoid repeating sensitive details in any moderation record. Retaliation
against a person for raising a concern is unacceptable.

Maintainers enforce these standards for the community; they do not claim
control over external repositories.
