Future covers are optional. A maintainer must review a public or
permissioned image, check that it contains no personal or private
information, remove unnecessary embedded personal metadata, and save it
locally under `docs/assets/projects/`.
Record its source and license or permission here. Never hotlink or
automatically download an image from submitted URLs, and do not use
unreviewed SVGs or active content.
